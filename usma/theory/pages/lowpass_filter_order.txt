# LP Order

**Parameter:** `lowpass_filter_order` (FRF) / `psd_lowpass_filter_order` (PSD)
**Default:** `7`

## What it is

The order of the Butterworth filter passed to
`scipy.signal.butter(order, cutoff, btype='low', output='sos')`.

## Implementation note — effective order is doubled

USMA uses `sosfiltfilt()` (second-order sections with zero-phase filtering).
`sosfiltfilt` applies the filter **twice** — forward and backward — giving
an effective order of `2 × lp_order`. At the default order 7, the effective
roll-off is equivalent to a 14th-order filter.

## Why `sosfiltfilt` instead of `filtfilt(b, a, ...)`

The older `filtfilt(b, a, ...)` approach suffers from ill-conditioned
polynomial coefficients at high orders and low cutoffs. This was causing
numerical instability in USMA's analysis at orders ≥ 7.

`sosfiltfilt` is **numerically stable at any order**. The Order spinbox is
now functional across its full range — but it remains a low-impact tuning
knob because the filter is already very steep at order ≥ 5.

## Effective order / attenuation table

```
| Order | Effective order (sosfiltfilt) | Attenuation at 2x cutoff |
|-------|-------------------------------|--------------------------|
|   3   |              6                |       ~-72 dB            |
|   5   |             10                |       ~-120 dB           |
|   7   |             14                |       ~-168 dB           |
|   9   |             18                |       ~-216 dB           |
```

At the default `lp_cutoff = 0.07`, doubling the frequency to `0.14` is
already deep in the stopband regardless of order. The residual shape is
dominated by the **cutoff frequency** choice, not the order.

## Practical guidance

**Order is not a useful tuning knob at these settings.** Leave it at 7.

The effective controls for LP behaviour, in order of impact, are:

1. `lp_cutoff`
2. `relative_residual_ratio`
3. `exceedance_ratio_threshold`

If you find the LP method misbehaving, adjust those three parameters —
changing the filter order will produce almost no visible effect.

## Why LP Order "seems to do nothing"

In an ideal LP filter with perfect roll-off, order is irrelevant — any
order ≥ 1 gives the same result (a hard cutoff). Real Butterworth filters
are not perfect, so order matters *at the transition band*. But once the
effective order is ≥ 10 (i.e. `lp_order ≥ 5`), the transition band is
already narrow enough that further increases are cosmetic. The default of 7
places you well inside this regime.
